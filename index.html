Services

Six practice areas, one goal: data you can trust and use.

01

Data Engineering

Reliable foundations for everything else. Pipelines, models and platforms that move data from where it is created to where it is needed.

  • Batch and real-time streaming pipelines
  • Warehouse modelling: facts, dimensions, point-in-time snapshots
  • On-prem to cloud migration and cost optimisation
  • CI/CD and release engineering for data
02

AI & Machine Learning

AI features that hold up in production, with the evaluation and guardrails needed to trust what they say.

  • Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines
  • Agentic workflows and internal chatbots
  • LLM evaluation: human-in-the-loop review and LLM judges
  • ML infrastructure and AI opportunity discovery
03

Data Science & Analytics

Turning well-modelled data into decisions. Sharper questions, metrics that matter and monitoring that catches change early.

  • Metrics, KPIs and executive dashboards
  • Time-series monitoring of business vital signs
  • Customer journey and service-usage analysis
  • Performance and cost analytics
04

Data Quality & Governance

Data people can rely on, and that stands up to audit. Quality is designed in and tested continuously, not inspected afterwards.

  • Automated data quality assessment frameworks
  • Unit, sample and UAT testing for data pipelines
  • Privacy, financial-reporting and data-residency compliance
  • Lineage, discovery and master data management
05

Data Products & Platforms

Data that people actually use. Tools and platforms that let both technical and non-technical teams serve themselves.

  • Self-serve exploration and BI tooling at scale
  • Declarative pipeline platforms with GUI, API and CLI
  • No-code builders for non-technical users
  • Adopting open-source tools securely
06

Architecture & Leadership

Senior guidance when the direction matters as much as the code: choosing the stack, sequencing the work and growing the team.

  • Technical vision and multi-team roadmaps
  • Technology selection and phased delivery plans
  • Advising senior leadership
  • Mentoring, documentation and team training

Selected work

What that looks like in practice.

A sample of problems our principal has led or delivered, across e-commerce, health technology and the federal public sector.

Health technology

AI interpretation in production

Led a team of engineers building AI-powered software that interprets lab results for practitioners, with thousands of results processed in production. Combined RAG pipelines and agentic workflows with human-in-the-loop validation and LLM judges to keep outputs accurate and within product and legal limits.

Federal public sector

A data quality programme from scratch

Designed and built an end-to-end framework to create, validate and run data quality assessments: 200+ active monitoring rules across 30+ datasets, automated through CI/CD. Advised leadership on the technology stack, then documented the architecture and trained the developers who now maintain it.

Global e-commerce

Monitoring a business’s vital signs

Built an end-to-end data exploration tool that generated, monitored and visualised 3.2 million time-series data points a day, used by more than 3,500 people. Managed the ten-person team of data scientists, developers and DevOps engineers behind it.

Data platform

A declarative platform for pipelines

Designed and built an extensible platform for defining data pipelines declaratively, with reusable components across engines and GUI, API and CLI interfaces. Introduced an open-source BI tool to the organisation, made security compliant and scalable for big data.

Privacy & compliance

Five petabytes, one deadline

Led an organisation-wide effort to bring 5 petabytes of unstructured data into privacy compliance, migrating and cleaning thousands of datasets. Also delivered audit-ready financial reporting and data assets designed around Canada–US data residency rules.

Cloud & performance

Faster pipelines, lower bills

Measured the impact of an on-prem to cloud migration and built automated cost and usage auditing that saved roughly $1M. Re-engineered a business-critical dataset processing 80 million rows an hour to run three times faster.
