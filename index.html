How we work

Three ways to work with us.

Start small or start big. Many clients begin with an assessment and continue into delivery.

Assess

Data & AI assessment

Best when: You know something is wrong, but not what to fix first.

We review your data, pipelines, tools and team practices, then tell you plainly what we found.

  • Findings in plain language
  • Risks and quick wins
  • A prioritized roadmap

Build

Project delivery

Best when: You have a defined problem and need it solved properly.

We design, build and ship the solution end to end, working alongside your team in small increments.

  • A working, tested solution
  • Documentation and runbooks
  • Your team trained to own it

Advise

Embedded advisor

Best when: Your team is capable but needs senior direction.

We join part-time as your senior data and AI lead: reviewing designs, unblocking decisions and mentoring.

  • Architecture and design reviews
  • Technical leadership on demand
  • Mentoring for your engineers

  1. Understand

    We start with your business and the questions you need answered, and map the data challenges standing in the way.

  2. Build

    We deliver in small, working increments, so you see value early and can steer as priorities change.

  3. Hand over

    We document, explain and train as we go, so your team can run and extend what we leave behind.
