Services

Three practice areas, one goal: data you can trust and use.

01

Data Engineering

Reliable foundations for everything else. We design and build the pipelines, models and platforms that move data from where it is created to where it is needed.

  • Batch and streaming pipelines
  • Warehouse and lakehouse modelling
  • Data quality, testing and observability
02

Data Science

Turning well-modelled data into decisions. We help you ask sharper questions, measure what matters and put analysis and models into production.

  • Analytics and experimentation
  • Forecasting and machine learning
  • Metric definition and measurement
03

Data Product

Data that people actually use. We shape datasets, dashboards and data-powered features as products, with clear owners, users and outcomes.

  • Data strategy and roadmaps
  • Self-serve reporting and dashboards
  • Data-powered product features

Approach

Clarity first. Then delivery.

  1. Understand

    We start with your business and the questions you need answered, and map the data challenges standing in the way.

  2. Build

    We deliver in small, working increments, so you see value early and can steer as priorities change.

  3. Hand over

    We document, explain and train as we go. The aim is a team that fully understands its own data, not one that depends on us.
