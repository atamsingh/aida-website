How we work

Three ways to work with us.

Start small or start big. An assessment is the lowest-risk way to begin.

Assess

AI & data assessment

Best when: You want to use AI, but aren’t sure where or whether your data is ready.

We review your processes, data and tools, then tell you plainly where AI will pay off and what has to be fixed first.

  • A ranked list of AI opportunities
  • Data gaps, risks and quick wins
  • A prioritized roadmap

Build

Project delivery

Best when: You have a defined use case and need it built properly.

We design, build and ship the solution end to end, working alongside your team in small increments.

  • A working, tested solution
  • Documentation and runbooks
  • Your team trained to own it

Advise

Embedded advisor

Best when: Your team is capable but needs senior AI and data direction.

We join part-time as your senior AI and data lead: reviewing designs, unblocking decisions and mentoring.

  • Architecture and design reviews
  • Technical leadership on demand
  • Mentoring for your engineers
