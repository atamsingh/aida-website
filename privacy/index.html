Privacy

How we handle your information

Last updated November 25, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what personal information this website collects, why, and who handles it.

Who we are

AIDA Consulting Solutions is an AI and data consultancy based in Ottawa, Ontario. Atam Singh, the founder, is responsible for how personal information is handled.

What we collect, and why

  • When you send the contact form: your name, your email address, the topic you choose and your message, plus your organization and phone number if you add them. We use these only to reply to you and to discuss possible work with you.
  • When you book a call: the booking is made through Calendly, which asks for your name, your email address and anything you choose to add.
  • When you contact us another way, such as on LinkedIn: whatever you choose to send.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Other companies that handle it for us

We rely on a small number of services to run this site and answer you. Each handles information under its own privacy policy.

  • Web3Forms delivers contact form messages to us by email.
  • Google provides our email, and a backup copy of each contact form message is kept in Google Forms.
  • Calendly handles call bookings.
  • GitHub hosts this website. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and basic browser details when you load a page.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google when a page loads.

Some of these companies store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we are in contact with you, and afterwards as part of our business records. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Send the request through the contact form or to Atam on LinkedIn, and we will respond.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle a request, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
