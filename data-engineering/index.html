How it works

Diagnose. Rebuild. Automate.

  1. Diagnose

    We trace your pipelines end to end and measure where the time, cost and failures actually come from.

  2. Rebuild

    We fix the highest-impact pieces first, in small increments you can verify against the old results.

  3. Automate

    We add the tests, deployment and monitoring that keep it working, and train your team to run them.

Questions

What people ask us first.

Led by Atam Singh, a principal engineer with 10+ years in data and AI and experience at Stripe, Shopify and Fullscript. You work with him directly.

Which tools do you work with?

Most often dbt, Airflow, PySpark, Trino/Presto, BigQuery and PostgreSQL, on GCP or AWS, with Terraform and Kubernetes underneath. We work with the stack you have and only recommend changing it when there is a clear reason.

Can you fix what we have, or do you always rebuild?

We start by measuring what you have. Often a few targeted fixes to the worst pipelines deliver most of the benefit. We recommend a rebuild only when patching would cost more.

We are not doing AI. Is this still for us?

Yes. Reliable pipelines pay for themselves through time saved, lower cloud costs and numbers people trust. If AI comes later, your data will already be ready for it.

Do you work with our team or replace it?

With it. We work alongside your engineers and analysts, and leave documentation, tests and training behind so the work stays maintained.
