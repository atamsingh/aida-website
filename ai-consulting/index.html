Questions

What people ask us first.

Led by Atam Singh, a principal engineer with 10+ years in data and AI and experience at Stripe, Shopify and Fullscript. You work with him directly.

We’re a small team. Is AI consulting worth it for us?

Often the best first project is a small one: a single repetitive process that eats hours every week. The assessment tells you whether there is a real return. If the honest answer is “not yet”, we will say so.

Does our data need to be in good shape first?

No. Finding out what state your data is in is part of the assessment. If it needs work before AI will succeed, we will tell you exactly what, and we can do that work too.

Which AI tools and models do you use?

We are vendor-neutral. We have built with OpenAI models, open-source models from Hugging Face, and frameworks such as LangGraph, LangChain and Pydantic AI, and we recommend what fits your needs and budget.

How do you handle privacy and sensitive data?

Privacy and data-residency requirements are designed in from the start. Our principal has delivered data systems under Canada–US data residency restrictions and large-scale privacy compliance programmes.
