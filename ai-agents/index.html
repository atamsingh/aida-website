Questions

What people ask us first.

Led by Atam Singh, a principal engineer with 10+ years in data and AI and experience at Stripe, Shopify and Fullscript. You work with him directly.

How do you stop it making things up?

Three ways: grounding answers in your own data through retrieval, measuring accuracy against a test set built from real questions, and keeping a person in the loop where a wrong answer would be costly.

Which models and frameworks do you use?

Whatever fits. We have built with OpenAI models, open-source models from Hugging Face, and frameworks including LangGraph, LangChain and Pydantic AI. We will recommend based on your accuracy, cost and privacy needs.

Can it work with our existing systems?

Usually, yes. Assistants and agents are most useful when connected to the databases, documents and APIs you already have. Working out those connections is part of the first conversation.

We already have a prototype. Can you take it over?

Yes. We start by measuring how it performs today, then fix what is holding it back, which is often the data and retrieval and not the model.
